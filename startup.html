Startup fit

I built a quant fund's entire technology with three people, and a treasury platform alone. I know what building from nothing looks like.

Event Horizon Partners

Three people, eight years, now closed. We built a systematic futures trading platform from scratch: model training and orchestration across supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning, live execution, investor operations, risk management. Continuous experimentation at scale, champion versus challenger promotion, stability gates, walk-forward and regime testing, strict train and out-of-sample separation. Every trade and decision retained so the system's exhaust became the next version's training set.

I built the whole technology stack. Python, C#, TypeScript, BigQuery, DuckDB, Docker, Terraform, Ray Tune. Proof of concept first, then production, every time. That is how the whole platform was built.

Building treasury from scratch

I built a production treasury and payment operations platform in 3 months. Double-entry immutable ledger, multi-rail payments (ACH, Wire, RTP), JPMorgan Chase Wallet API integration with V2V transfers and virtual accounts, Stripe checkout, Plaid bank verification, reconciliation engine, approval workflows, waterfall revenue distribution, webhook delivery, QuickBooks GL sync.

31 tables. Provider abstraction so a second processor is configuration, not a rewrite. Two-phase payment pattern that keeps the ledger consistent even when provider calls fail. Reconciliation one to one, one to many and many to one, with auto-matching and an audit trail. All 23 ACH return codes matched to the original payment and reversed against the ledger automatically. One domain model served to people, workflows and agents.

FastAPI, PostgreSQL, SQLAlchemy, Terraform, GCP Cloud Run. The kind of thing Modern Treasury sells as a product. I built it as infrastructure.

Why startups
