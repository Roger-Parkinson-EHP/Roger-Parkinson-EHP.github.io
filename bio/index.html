The problem I work on

The most important unsolved problem in this field is how an enterprise feeds its intellectual capital to agents in the most consumable form. Models and harnesses are commodities; the context an organization has accumulated is not, and the question every organization has to ask is how much of it they are hiding from their agents behind an obstacle course of tool calls instead of a query.

My answer is a graph, and I measure it. Every conversation, pull request, doc and code change is captured continuously into a queryable, linked record: objects, links, actions and decision lineage, searchable by keyword and by meaning with a code graph beside it. An agent pulls a structured, bounded picture of the problem into context the same way after every reset. Every answer states its coverage and freshness, so the agent can tell what it has not seen. A fixed probe set scores retrieval before and after every change. Two numbers decide agent effectiveness: how long it takes to get the full picture, and the quality of the feedback loop from there.

How I get there

  • Proof of concept first, then production. That is how the fund's whole platform was built and how the treasury platform shipped in three months.
  • Context curated into a graph, so the agent queries once instead of scrambling through tool calls.
  • Measured, not asserted: probe sets before and after every change, ablations that reproduce.
  • Compliance treated as deterministic: it can be 100 percent, so be excellent at it and spend the freed attention on the probabilistic work.
  • Full-stack shipping with no handoff: Python, TypeScript, C#, SQL; PostgreSQL, BigQuery, DuckDB; Terraform, GitHub Actions; GCP, AWS, Cloudflare.
  • Evaluation, provenance and guardrails built in at the start. Bolted on at the end they are theater, and theater costs you your speed.
